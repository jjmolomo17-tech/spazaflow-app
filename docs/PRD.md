# Product Requirements Document (PRD) — SpazaFlow

## 1. User Roles & Permissions
1. **Owner (Admin):** Full access to add/edit/delete inventory, view profit margins, manage staff accounts, and clear customer debts.
2. **Staff (Cashier):** Can log sales, add items to existing customer credit tabs, and view stock counts (cannot delete products or view net profit).

## 2. Must-Have Features (Day One)
1. **Authentication & Role-Based Access:** Secure login enforcing Owner vs. Staff database permissions.
2. **Quick Inventory Manager:** Add products with cost price, selling price, stock quantity, and expiry date, with automatic "Low Stock" badges.
3. **Fast Sale & Stock Deduction:** One-tap sale logging that automatically deducts stock count in real time.
4. **Customer Credit Ledger ("The Book"):** Create customer profiles, log items bought on credit, record partial/full repayments, and view total owed.
5. **Owner Dashboard:** Daily summary showing total cash sales, total credit issued, and items needing restock.
