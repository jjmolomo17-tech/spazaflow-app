# Business Case: SpazaFlow

## The Problem
Local spaza shop and tuckshop owners track inventory and customer "book" credit on paper notebooks. Notebooks get lost, damaged, or miscalculated, causing owners to run out of top-selling items, lose money on expired goods, and fail to collect unpaid customer tabs—costing small shops thousands of Rands every month.

## The Customer
- **Primary User & Payer:** Independent spaza shop, tuckshop, and small convenience store owners in South Africa.
- **Secondary User:** Shop cashiers/assistants logging daily sales.

## The Solution
SpazaFlow is a simple, mobile-first web app that tracks low-stock alerts, expiry dates, and digital customer credit tabs in seconds.

## The Impact
- **Change:** Shop owners gain real-time visibility into profit, stock levels, and who owes them money.
- **Metrics:** 30% reduction in expired stock waste and 50% faster recovery of customer credit tabs within 60 days.

## The Money (Business Model)
Freemium SaaS subscription:
- **Free Tier:** Up to 50 products and 20 active customer tabs.
- **Pro Tier (R99/month):** Unlimited products, WhatsApp payment reminders for customer tabs, and daily profit reports.

## Go-To-Market (First 100 Customers)
1. Direct foot-canvassing at local taxi ranks and township high streets in Gauteng, onboarding owners in person in 5 minutes.
2. Partnering with local cash-and-carry wholesalers to hand out flyers at checkout.
3. Offering the first 3 months of Pro free to early adopters who refer two neighboring shop owners.

## Competition & Why Us
- **Paper notebooks:** Free, but easily lost, no alerts, and prone to math errors.
- **Enterprise POS systems (e.g., GAAP, Pilot):** Expensive hardware, complex setup, and high monthly fees.
- **Why Us:** Zero hardware required (runs on any budget Android smartphone), works cleanly on low data, and includes a built-in customer credit ("book") tracker tailored to township retail.
